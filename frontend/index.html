Pillar 01

Decentralized Clinical Trials (DCT)

Removes the burden of hospital visits and intimidating laboratory rigs. Patients participate in longitudinal neurological studies from the comfort of their home using ubiquitous webcams and wireless ECG sensors.

Remote Access 100% Browser-Based
Pillar 02

Autonomic Stress Quantification

Live Beat-to-Beat RR intervals continuously compute rolling RMSSD (Heart Rate Variability) from a Polar H10 chest strap, instantaneously detecting sympathetic stress surges and vagal withdrawal.

ECG Synchronization ±1 ms RR Precision
Pillar 03

Sub-Millimeter Motion Tracking

A 468-point optical mesh tracks nose-tip and head velocity at 30 FPS without placing physical sensors on the head, accurately extracting 2–5 Hz sinusoidal oscillatory tremor waveforms.

Optical Mesh 30 FPS Velocity Capture
Pillar 04

Clinician Video Adjudication

When stress-correlated tremor episodes occur, an automated 10-second circular video buffer is preserved. Neurologists inspect synchronized slow-motion video to verify true nystagmus vs everyday motion artifacts.

Verified Precision 88.9% True Positive Rate
